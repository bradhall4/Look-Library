Look Library
 

No more mid work.

You’ve got the idea. You’ve also got nine hours and a list of features. So you reach for whatever imagery is close enough. And the client wants tight mocks. And the thing that was sharp in your head turns into lukewarm slop oatmeal. Resist the oatmeal.

  1. Here’s a shelf of looks that are already solved. Not moodboards, recipes.
  2. Pull one off the shelf when it fits the concept.
  3. Cook a new one when the concept demands it.
  4. Walk into the pitch with the look nailed, instead of asking the room to squint and imagine it.
  5. Then hire the artist to actually shoot it, and let them bring the human magic no recipe ever will.
